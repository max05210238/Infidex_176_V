# Build Video Transcript

Transcript of Denis Aminev's Infidex IV-to-V build video, organized by chapter with timestamps. Lightly cleaned for readability; the designer narrated it as improvised live commentary. Read the PDF manual for everything not covered here.

---

## 1. Intro (0:21)

Assembling the new Infidex V camera. This one is full improvisation, not scripted like the earlier videos. Plan: disassemble an Infidex IV and reuse its optical viewfinder and Mamiya 80mm lens.

## 2. Materials and tools (1:06)

Walks through the essential materials and tools laid out, plus a second table of "non-essential" items. Foam is essential. Open the assembly guide first. Calipers, mislabeled as not essential, are needed.

## 3. Checking dimensions (1:34)

- Whole camera thickness should be 37.8 mm, measured mid-side. Measured: 37.51 mm. Acceptable for FFF/FDM.
- Model height should be 36.03 mm. Measured: ~36 mm. Good to proceed.

## 4. Body assembly (2:17)

Check part fitment. A small gap between halves is fine. Chisel off bumps and flatten with sandpaper, scraping carefully (sharp edges). Check the smaller parts fit too; remove a few bumps where needed.

The taking spool shaft (step 6 in the manual) goes in at an angle first, then upright, then pushed up. Place the shaft holder inside before gluing. File any stiffness until smooth.

There is a pocket at the top right for a gear; it should sit and rotate freely. The sprocket shaft is placed from the top and located in its slot.

Before gluing, place the printed pressure plate on its position as a guide. It moves freely, that is fine. It ensures the two film rails stay flat and parallel while the body halves are glued, which is critical. Do not rush. Press the glued halves together for a few seconds.

A small bump indicates the bottom side of the helicoid mount.

## 5. Helicoid assembly (5:54)

Prepare surfaces and check fit. The thread should screw in and out freely. Snap the fixation ring into its groove so it rotates freely.

Glue the focus ring to the snapped fixation ring, not to the mount, body, or your fingers. Do not apply glue to the focus ring itself, or pushing it on will bond it to the helicoid mount. Apply a decent but mild amount of superglue to the fixation ring.

The mount has two slide guides for the inner-ring rails. Engage them, then rotate clockwise to thread it in.

Only the thread holds the assembly together, so be careful when focusing close. The camera can do mild macro, but unscrewing too far drops the lens out.

Glue the inner thread to the lens board, aligning the matching marks on both parts. Press mildly.

## 6. Door installation (9:12)

The door will not fit at first. Remove material from the hinge points and edges. The lower ledge was printed with supports and ends up slightly thick, so remove a little material until the door fits.

## 7. Brass thread inserts (10:14)

The soldering iron is hot, be careful. Apply heat slowly to seat the inserts. One M3 insert done. The larger 1/4" thread hole is easier to seat than the small M3 ones.

## 8. Spring and hinge wires (11:13)

The hinge uses rigid stainless wire, about 0.62 mm, that goes straight in.

The spring seats inside the body and acts as a stopper for the rewind knob. Form it by making one coil around something thin (an M3 screw, or better a plain nail). Push the coil into place with a file. When seated correctly, pulling the knob shows the spring working as a stopper. This part is hard. ("I cried only three times.")

The winding knob and counter go on after the paint job.

## 9. Pressure plate (12:54)

Line the pressure plate with black flocked paper. It must be black, not light gray or white, or images get halation everywhere. Cut to size and glue on. Matte black vinyl also works; do not use glossy. Apply self-adhesive flocking inside the chamber too. This is easier before the body halves are glued. The cold shoe was installed off-camera.

## 10. Lens installation (13:56)

When transferring the long thread from the disassembled Infidex IV, it may not fit at first; work it until it seats.

## 11. Marking distances on the helicoid (14:44)

Focus on a target at a known distance and mark the helicoid.

- 2 m mark made by focusing on a target exactly 2 m away.
- Then 5 m, 10 m, 15 m, 20 m, and 31 m to infinity.

Camera is then painted and fully functional.

## 12. A little talk (15:52)

There was a small light leak in the lens board. It was found and fixed in the STL files (link in the original video description). Read the assembly guide first; a lot is not covered in the video.

## 13 to 15. Test shots and outro (16:11)

Test images shown. Thanks to viewers. Plus a t-shirt a friend gifted.
