## What is this?

The **Infidex 176 V** is a fully open-source, 3D-printed panoramic 35mm film camera designed by Denis Aminev. It shoots a unique 3:1 panoramic frame (72 × 24 mm) on standard 35mm film using a Mamiya TLR taking lens, producing stunning ultra-wide images.

Denis designed, built, documented, and shared this project for free on his website [timetowaste.ru](https://timetowaste.ru). He is the sole creator — I am just helping to keep it available.

---

## Why this reupload?

Denis announced that his website will shut down on **21 March 2027**. His exact words:

> *"This website will disappear on 21 March 2027. Project brought to a point where I am satisfied with the results and I will no longer pay for this domain. Feel free to distribute files of the project on any other website or a platform. Thank you. Peace."*

I want to make sure this incredible project doesn't disappear. I tried to contact Denis directly — I left comments on his build video and searched for other contact info — but received no response. Following his explicit permission to redistribute, I'm uploading this mirror so the community can continue to access, build, and enjoy the Infidex 176 V.

**If Denis or anyone from his team sees this and wants changes made, please reach out — I will immediately comply.**

---

## What's in the download?

The ZIP file contains everything you need:

**STL files (57 parts, print-ready):**
- Body shells (top/bottom split, unibody, 4 door variants)
- Lens mount — Focusing Helicoid (recommended, includes Blue Dot shorter variants)
- Lens mount — Classic Cone (fixed focus)
- Lens mount — Tube (fixed focus)
- Lens mount — Old Version cone for T-22 lens
- Small parts (knobs, gears, viewfinders, lens hoods, lens cap, cold shoe, etc.)

**FBX mesh exports (61 files):**
- For viewing/editing in 3D software other than 3ds Max
- Includes Kodak cone parts and metal part references (.dae)

**Community mods (14 files):**
- Modified body, doors, and helicoid mount base (STL + FBX)

**Also included:**
- `Infidex 176 V manual.pdf` — the original assembly and printing guide by Denis
- `Infidex 176 V_NEWEST.max` — 3ds Max master CAD source file

## Print settings (designer's ABS profile)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Nozzle | 0.4 mm |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |
| Inner perimeters | 4 (important for rigidity) |
| Infill | 25% max |

ABS, PLA, and PETG all work. Print the **body first** to check dimensions before printing everything else. See the included PDF manual for full details.

## Required materials (besides printed parts)

- Mamiya TLR taking lens (80mm recommended)
- Superglue (two-component with activator preferred)
- Rigid stainless steel wire, 0.6–0.8 mm
- Black flocked paper or matte black vinyl
- Black foam for light seals
- Double-sided tape (non-foam)
- Basic tools: wire nippers, calipers, sandpaper, spanner wrench
